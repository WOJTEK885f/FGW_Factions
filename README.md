**F:GW Factions** is part of the 7BOW Group Factions project, which aims to allow missionmakers and other content creators to bring diverse and unconventional factions into their scenarios, providing new possibilities for creating interesting conflicts and stories within Arma 3.

This project intends to recreate the entire geopolitical conflict of the world of *Cherniv*. It ports the distinct military, paramilitary, and rebel forces from the game *Freeman: Guerrilla Warfare* into Arma 3. The mod delivers faithfully reproduced factions equipped with custom character identities, authentic gear and specialized Eden Editor and Zeus groups, providing the tools needed to build your scenarios smoothly.

## Key Features

* **Authentic Units:** Based directly on the game, with matching loadouts and Cherniv-inspired name, face, and voice pools.
* **Companion System:** Unique named characters with persistent identities, facial features, custom gear, and predefined group roles.
* **Faction-Based Groups:**
  * Eden and Zeus groups reflecting F:GW predefined squads as well as map armies,
  * Unique companion-led variants, matching each character's playstyle and fighting capabilities.
* **Custom Gear:** Hybrid gear combining existing visual assets with custom configs to match the in-game equipment appearance.
* **Modular Approach:** Optional dependencies let you unload mods required by factions you won't use without causing issues.
